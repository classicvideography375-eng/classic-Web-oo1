                A FEW IDEAS TO GET YOU STARTED

                Find your moment.

                Use these as a starting point, then make each pose your own. The best photos feel natural, not perfect.

                Weddings

                Open the Pinterest pose idea ↗
                • Walk together and whisper something that makes you laugh.
                • Hold hands and take a quiet moment away from the crowd.
                • Share a slow spin, letting the outfit move naturally.
                • Gather your wedding party for a relaxed, close-in group portrait.

                Portraits

                See portraits on Instagram ↗
                • Walk toward the camera, looking around rather than straight ahead.
                • Sit comfortably, lean forward slightly, and rest your hands naturally.
                • Look over your shoulder toward the light, then turn back with a smile.
                • Bring an object you love and interact with it while chatting.

                Events

                • Make a loose circle with friends and share a story together.
                • Raise a glass for a candid toast, then capture the reactions.
                • Move across the dance floor with your group between songs.
                • Pause with a guest to greet them and catch a genuine moment.

                Brand shoots

                • Show a real step in your process, from hands at work to the finished detail.
                • Talk with a teammate while doing something you normally do together.
                • Set up your workspace, then capture a natural moment in the environment.
                • Hold or use your product in context instead of posing with it toward camera.
